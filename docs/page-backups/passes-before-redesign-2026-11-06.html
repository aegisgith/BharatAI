Before you pay

Which pass should I buy?

Coming for the conference: the Delegate Pass. Want a hands-on workshop seat as well: Premium Delegate. Want the Bharat AI Leaders’ Evening, the lounge and the speaker meet & greet: VIP. A student or faculty member with ID: Academic. Only the exhibition floor: the free Visitor Pass, where entry is not guaranteed: it depends on slot availability on 20 and 21 Nov, a half-day slot or none, confirmed by email.

Is entry guaranteed?

On every paid pass, yes, on both days. Entry on a free Visitor Pass is not guaranteed. It depends on slot availability on 20 and 21 Nov: you may be offered a half-day slot, or none. We confirm by email before the event. Every paid pass has guaranteed entry on both days.

What does the price include?

Prices are + 18% GST. Prices went up on 6 Nov and may go up again. An order keeps the price it was made at.

Can I get a GST invoice in my company’s name?

Yes. The paid form has a section for the registered company name, GSTIN and billing address. A receipt is emailed the moment the payment goes through, and the GST invoice follows from the accounts team.

How do I pay?

On this site, through CCAvenue: UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and wallets. Your pass is confirmed the moment the payment goes through and you are brought straight back here.

What if I can’t come after all?

Transfer the pass to a colleague free of charge, or take a credit note for 100% of the amount paid, until 13 Nov 2026. Cash refunds follow the refund policy.

How do workshop seats work?

One hands-on workshop comes with the Premium Delegate and VIP passes. Seats are limited and go first come, first served: after you pay, you choose your seat in the app.

We are a team of five or more

Teams of five or more: five passes for the price of four (Delegate or Premium Delegate), on request. Ask for a group rate.

I already hold a paid pass. Can I move up?

Yes. A confirmed Delegate, Premium Delegate or Academic pass moves up to any dearer pass, and you pay only the difference between the two prices. Open the app, sign in, and tap Upgrade my pass on your pass card. It shows the difference before you pay.
