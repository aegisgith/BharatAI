Propose Your Campus

What a Campus Panel Looks Like

One repeatable 90-minute format, adapted to the host institution's disciplines.

A real panel

Three to five practitioners from industry, academia and policy — not a product pitch, and not a single-speaker lecture.

Open floor

A meaningful share of the time goes to student questions. The unscripted part is usually the part people remember.

Practical takeaways

Concrete guidance on skills, portfolios and career paths, grounded in what employers are actually hiring for right now.

A route to November

Attendees carry the thread into the main conference at WTC Mumbai, where each theme is explored across two full days.

For Institutions

Host a pre-event panel at your campus
