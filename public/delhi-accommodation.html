Getting to Bharat Mandapam

  • By metroThe nearest station is Supreme Court, once called Pragati Maidan, on the Blue Line. The venue is about 500 metres away, a short walk.
  • From the airportIndira Gandhi International Airport is about 16 km by road from Terminal 1, and about 22 km from Terminals 2 and 3. Allow an hour at busy times.
  • The venueBharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, New Delhi. Get directions

Coming from outside India

Check which visa you need on the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in. If your application asks for a letter from the organisers, ask us for one. Please wait for your visa before booking travel you cannot cancel. India Standard Time is UTC+5:30.
