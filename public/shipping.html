Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

This policy explains how Aegis Knowledge Trust delivers what you buy from Bharat AI Innovation Conference & Exhibition 2026, held at World Trade Center Mumbai on 20–21 November 2026.

We do not ship physical goods. Everything sold on this website — conference passes, exhibition booths, sponsorship packages and workshop seats — is delivered electronically by email, and access is granted in person at the venue. No courier or postal delivery is involved, and no shipping charges are levied.

1. What you receive

  • Conference passes (Visitor, Delegate, VIP, Academic): an email confirmation containing your registration reference, the pass type purchased, and instructions for collecting your badge at the venue.
  • Exhibition booths: a booking confirmation and an Exhibitor Agreement setting out your stand allocation and entitlements.
  • Sponsorship packages: a confirmation and a Sponsor Agreement setting out the agreed deliverables.
  • Workshops and training: an email confirmation with the session details and joining instructions.

2. Delivery timeline

Confirmation of your order is sent to the email address given at registration once payment has been successfully authorised by our payment partner. This is normally immediate, and in all cases within 24 hours of payment confirmation.

Where a payment is placed under review by the payment gateway or the issuing bank, delivery follows the outcome of that review. We will contact you if a payment cannot be confirmed.

3. Badge collection at the venue

Physical badges are not posted. Your badge is issued at the registration desk at World Trade Center Mumbai on the event dates, on production of your confirmation email and a government-issued photo ID. Registration opens at 09:00 IST on both days.

4. Delivery address

The only delivery address we require is a valid email address. Please make sure it is entered correctly at registration, as confirmations are sent to that address and cannot be delivered otherwise. A postal address is collected only where it is required for GST invoicing.

5. If you do not receive your confirmation

If nothing has arrived within 24 hours of payment, please first check your spam, promotions and quarantine folders, as automated confirmations are sometimes filtered there. If it is still missing, contact us with the payment reference and the email address used, and we will resend it.

  • Email info@bharataiinnovation.com
  • Call +91 89765 80367 (Mon–Sat, 10:00–18:00 IST)

We will respond within one business day.

6. International participants

Delivery is electronic and identical regardless of where you are located. Attendance is in person at World Trade Center Mumbai; we do not arrange travel, visas or accommodation, though visa invitation letters can be requested at the address above.

7. Cancellations and refunds

Delivery of a pass does not affect your cancellation rights. Transfers, refund eligibility and timelines are set out separately in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
