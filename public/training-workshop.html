Data Science to Agentic AI: A CXO’s Guide to Strategic Decision-Making

As organizations move from Data Science and Machine Learning to Generative AI and Agentic AI, CXOs face a critical challenge: identifying the right business problems, selecting high-value AI use cases, evaluating pilot outcomes, and scaling successful solutions into production.

This session gives business and technology leaders a practical framework for navigating the AI landscape, making informed technology and investment decisions, and turning AI opportunities into measurable business outcomes.

What the session covers

  • Identifying the right business problems: where AI can address meaningful business challenges and create measurable value.
  • Selecting and prioritizing AI use cases: when to use Data Science, Machine Learning, Generative AI or Agentic AI, based on business needs, feasibility and risk.
  • Designing and evaluating AI pilots: defining success metrics, establishing baselines, and evaluating technical performance and business impact.
  • Moving from pilot to production: assessing readiness for enterprise deployment, including integration, security, governance, scalability and operational reliability.
  • Scaling AI for business impact: a roadmap to prioritize investments, scale successful solutions, and realize sustained business value.
Key takeaway: a practical decision-making framework for CXOs to move from AI opportunities and pilot projects to reliable, scalable, production-ready enterprise solutions.

From Prototype to Production: Building Enterprise AI with Forward-Deployed Engineers

How do you turn a real business problem into an AI solution that can work in an enterprise? This 90-minute session introduces the Forward-Deployed Engineer (FDE) role and shows how business needs, AI engineering and enterprise integration come together. Through live coding, participants see an AI-powered workflow built, tested against realistic cases, and examined through the lens of production readiness.

What you will take away

  • What a Forward-Deployed Engineer does, and how the role differs from traditional software and AI engineering.
  • How an engineer translates a business problem into a scoped AI solution and architecture.
  • A live-coded enterprise AI workflow that connects an LLM with data, tools and business logic.
  • Why testing, failure handling, security, reliability and evaluation matter beyond a working prototype.

The 90 minutes

  1. The rise of the FDEWhat a Forward-Deployed Engineer is, why the role is emerging, and why enterprises need engineers who can connect business problems to working technology.
  2. The FDE mindsetHow FDEs discover the real problem, clarify requirements, define constraints, and identify what success should look like.
  3. Designing the solutionA concise walkthrough of the architecture: model, enterprise data, tools or APIs, workflow, and guardrails.
  4. Live coding: build an enterprise AI workflowA focused business workflow agent, such as a procurement assistant that checks policy and supporting information through a mock enterprise API, then recommends a next step.
  5. Test, find failures, improveRealistic test cases, including missing information or a policy exception. Examine the output, identify failure modes, and demonstrate an improvement.
  6. From prototype to productionWhat real deployment needs: access control, privacy, reliability, observability, evaluation, human approval, and integration.
  7. Key takeawaysThe FDE mindset and the capabilities required to build useful enterprise AI.
Who should attend: fresh engineering graduates, software developers, AI/ML engineers, data scientists, technology leaders, and enterprise decision-makers, including CIOs, CTOs and Chief Data Officers.

Automatic Speech Recognition (Speech-to-Text)

A 90-minute workshop on Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), also called Speech-to-Text (STT): the kinds of speech problems it solves, the popular models available today, and how to choose the right model and the right hardware for your task. It ends with a hands-on session.

What the 90 minutes cover

  1. Introduction to Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), or Speech-to-Text (STT)
  2. The various types of ASR and STT problems
  3. A short discussion of the popular models available
  4. From problem to solutionIdentifying the problem and developing a solution: selecting the right model or models for your task, selecting the right hardware for the model, optimization and trade-offs.
  5. A hands-on session
Who should attend: AI/ML enthusiasts, technical entrepreneurs, and others building products with speech.
