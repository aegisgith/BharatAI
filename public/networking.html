Bharat AI Connect · the networking app

Meet the right people, not just the crowd

A full hall is only useful if you find your ten. Bharat AI Connect is the event’s networking app: it matches you to the people worth your two days, and lets you lock in the meeting before you arrive.

AI Matchmaking

Ranked introductions based on your interests and role: founders matched to investors, exhibitors to buyers, researchers to collaborators.

Attendee Directory

Search delegates, speakers and investors by name, company, role or what they work on, and see who’s online.

Private Messages

Message people directly inside the app. No email address or phone number changes hands.

Book 1:1 Meetings

Propose a time, a length and a place. They accept or decline in the app, so you walk in with a diary instead of hoping to bump into the right person.

Your Agenda

Star the sessions, talks and workshops you want across both days and carry the plan on your phone.

Your Pass
