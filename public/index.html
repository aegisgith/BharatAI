Frequently Asked Questions

▸ What is Bharat AI Innovation 2026?

Bharat AI Innovation 2026 is India's B2B platform for AI, held Nov 20-21, 2026 at World Trade Center Mumbai. It puts the organisations already running AI in production, the companies providing it, and the policymakers shaping it in one building for two days — across a conference programme, an exhibition floor and private meeting space.

▸ What topics are covered at the AI conference?

The conference features 10+ specialized tracks including Generative AI (GenAI), Large Language Models (LLMs), AI for Healthcare, FinTech AI, Manufacturing AI, AgriTech innovations, Cloud & Infrastructure, Enterprise AI Solutions, AI Startups, and AI Research. We also have dedicated sessions on ChatGPT, AI chatbot development, LLM fine-tuning, and practical AI implementations.

▸ Who should attend this AI summit?

This event is ideal for AI researchers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, startup founders, enterprise leaders, CTOs, technology managers, investors, policymakers, students, and anyone interested in artificial intelligence and its applications. Whether you're exploring AI for your business or advancing your technical skills, this conference offers valuable insights and networking opportunities.

▸ What are the ticket prices for the conference?

We offer six pass types: Visitor Pass (Free) for exhibition and keynote access, where entry is not guaranteed: it depends on slot availability on 20 and 21 November, so you may be offered a half-day slot, or none, confirmed by email before the event; Academic Pass (INR 999 + GST) for students and researchers, Delegate Pass (INR 4,999 + GST) for the full conference, exhibition, materials and lunch, Premium Delegate Pass (INR 9,999 + GST) for everything in Delegate plus one hands-on workshop, VIP Pass (INR 19,999 + GST) for everything in Premium Delegate plus the CXO Gala Networking Evening on Day 1, VIP lounge, priority seating and speaker meet & greet, and Media Pass (By Invite) for accredited journalists and media partners. Every paid pass has guaranteed entry on both days. These are the standard prices until 5 November 2026; from 6 November the Academic Pass is INR 3,999, Delegate INR 8,999, Premium Delegate INR 14,999 and VIP INR 24,999 + GST. Workshop seats are limited and go first come, first served. Teams of five or more get five passes for the price of four (Delegate or Premium Delegate), on request. Compare every pass.

▸ How can I exhibit at the AI exhibition?

We offer 7 exhibition booth types to suit every budget: Startup Pod (2.25 sqm, ₹48,000 + GST), Explorer Booth (4 sqm, ₹1,29,000 + GST), Innovator Booth (6 sqm, ₹1,95,000 + GST), Accelerator Booth (9 sqm, ₹2,91,000 + GST), Enterprise Booth (8 sqm, ₹2,58,000 + GST), Flagship Pavilion (12 sqm, ₹3,87,000 + GST), and Mega Pavilion (53.9 sqm, ₹17,40,000 + GST). 93 booths across 8 industry zones. All packages include exhibitor passes, booth infrastructure, and marketing benefits. Contact us for custom packages.

▸ Are there training workshops available?

Yes! We offer hands-on training workshops led by industry experts covering GenAI, Agentic AI, VibeCoding, AI Product Development, Recommender System Development, AI Chatbot Development, LLM Model Fine-Tuning, and more. These practical sessions provide real-world skills you can immediately apply to your projects.

▸ Can startups pitch at the conference?

Absolutely! Our Innovation Stage provides startups with 8-minute pitch slots to showcase their AI innovations to investors, enterprise decision-makers, and industry leaders. This is an excellent opportunity to gain visibility, receive expert feedback, and connect with potential partners and customers.

▸ Where is the conference venue located?

The event takes place at World Trade Center Mumbai, Cuffe Parade, Mumbai, Maharashtra. The venue is centrally located with excellent connectivity, close to luxury hotels, and offers world-class conference facilities with stunning waterfront views.
