Coming from outside India

What to know before you book your travel.

Visa
Check which visa you need on the official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in. If your application asks for a letter from the organisers, ask us for one. Please wait for your visa before booking travel you cannot cancel.
From the airport
Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL) is about 18 km from Bharat Mandapam. Allow an hour by road at busy times.
By metro
The nearest station is Supreme Court, once called Pragati Maidan, on the Blue Line. The venue is a short walk away.
Time and money
India Standard Time is UTC+5:30. Passes are priced in Indian rupees, plus 18% GST.
Ask for a visa letter

Questions

When and where is it?

On Wednesday 28 and Thursday 29 July 2027, at Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan, New Delhi.

Can I get a pass for Delhi now?

Yes. Delegate, Premium Delegate, VIP and Academic passes are on sale now. The free Visitor Pass is exhibition only, with entry by time slot: register here to join the waitlist.

How do I book a booth, sponsor or speak?

For a booth, apply here and tell us what you will show. We review every application and reply within 2 working days; if approved, our team confirms your place and the next step for booking. We will publish the floor plan later. For a partnership, choose one and leave your details. To speak, choose Speak in the form.

What happens after I pay for a pass?

Your receipt comes by email straight away, and your GST invoice follows from the accounts team. Closer to the event we will email you your entry pass and open the app for Delhi.

Is this the same as the Mumbai event?

No, they are two separate events. Bharat AI Innovation 2026 is on 20–21 November 2026 at World Trade Center Mumbai. The Delhi edition is on 28–29 July 2027 at Bharat Mandapam. A pass for one does not cover the other.
