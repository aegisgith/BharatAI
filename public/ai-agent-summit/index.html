How the ten minutes work

Everyone gets the same clock, so the room can compare agents fairly. We run it the way live demo days run elsewhere: a timer on stage and a hard stop.

Minutes 0 to 7
Your agent, working live

Run a real task end to end in your product. One title slide is allowed. After that the screen is your product.

Minutes 7 to 10
Questions from the room

The panel and the audience ask what a buyer would ask in a sales call.

The timer is real
It runs at the podium and on screen, and the microphone goes off at 10:00.
Two podiums
The next company sets up while you present, so nobody loses time to changeovers.
Send a backup by 10 April
A screen recording we switch to only if your connection or product fails on the day.
A code on your slide
People scan it during your slot and their details come straight to your team.

Questions people ask us

Anything else, write to info@bharataiinnovation.com or call +91 89765 80367.

What exactly happens in a 10-minute slot?

Seven minutes of your agent doing a real task, live, then three minutes of questions from the panel and the audience. One title slide is allowed. The rest should be your product on screen.

Who can apply to showcase?

Any company with a working AI agent that customers, pilot users or real users rely on today. Startups, growth-stage companies, enterprises and global firms are all welcome. Ideas and mock-ups are not a fit for this stage.

How are companies selected?

An evaluation committee reviews applications every week and asks four questions. Does it work today? Does it complete real tasks? Does anyone use it? Can it be shown clearly in seven minutes? We reply within five working days.

When do we pay?

Only after you are accepted. We send an invoice with a payment link, and your slot is confirmed once payment is received within 10 days of acceptance.

What if our live demo fails on the day?

Send us a screen recording by 10 April 2027. If your connection or product fails on stage, our A/V team switches to it so your ten minutes are not lost.

Is GST extra?

Yes. All prices exclude 18% GST. Companies outside India are invoiced in rupees, and we can share US dollar equivalents on request.

What kinds of agent can apply?

Any kind. The examples on this page are only examples: if your agent finishes real tasks for real users, in any sector and any language, it can apply.

When will the running order be out?

The stage runs from 09:00 to 19:00, and the cocktail evening follows at 19:30. We will publish who is on stage, and when, once the showcase companies are confirmed. Registered attendees get it by email.

Is there a networking app?

Yes. Delegate, VIP, Buyer and Investor pass holders, showcase teams and speakers get Bharat AI Connect, the app we run for Bharat AI Innovation. Use it to find people, connect, message and book meetings at the venue. See how it works.

Do I need a visa to attend?

Most visitors from outside India do. Check which visa you need, and apply, on the Government of India's official site, indianvisaonline.gov.in, well before you travel. Hotels near the venue are listed under where to stay.

Can we sponsor instead of showcasing?

Yes. Partner packages start at ₹5,00,000 and combine a booth, stage time and branding, and add-ons start at ₹1,50,000. See partner packages.
