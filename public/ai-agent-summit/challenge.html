AI Agent Challenge

Build an AI agent that finishes a real task, online, from now to 28 February 2027. The evaluation committee picks five finalists, and they show their agents free on the main stage at WTC Mumbai on 16 April.

Register your team →

What to build

An agent that finishes a real task for an Indian business or public service, from start to end, rather than a chatbot that only answers questions. Choose your own problem, or pick one from the problem board, where companies post tasks they want an agent for.

Working, not slides
Send a video of your agent doing the task, under five minutes, and a link we can try.
Any model, any stack
Use whatever model, framework or cloud you like. What you build stays yours.
Small teams
One to four people: students, independent developers, and teams that have not raised outside funding.
Free to enter
There is no fee to register, to submit, or to present in the finals.

Dates

NowRegistration is openIt takes five minutes, and teams can start building as soon as they register.
31 January 2027Registration closesEvery team gets the full brief and how to submit by email.
28 February 2027Submissions closeA video of your agent at work, under five minutes, and a link to try it.
15 March 2027Five finalists namedChosen by the evaluation committee. Every team hears back by email.
16 April 2027Finals on the main stageFive minutes each at WTC Mumbai, at the end of the stage day. The winner is announced at the cocktail evening.

What the five finalists get

Five minutes on the main stage
Your agent, live, in front of the summit's audience, with no fee.
Two team passes
With lunch, the networking app and the cocktail evening, where the winner is announced.
A place in the agent directory
Next to the showcase companies, in the summit's directory of agents.

How we judge: the four questions we ask of every showcase application. Does it work? Does it finish a real task? Would someone use it? Can it be shown clearly in five minutes?
