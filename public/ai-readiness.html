The six AI-readiness pillars

From strategy and data to talent, governance, value and the GenAI/agentic frontier — each pillar mapped to India’s DPDP Act & IT Rules, the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO/IEC 42001. Geometric, non-compensatory scoring with critical-control gates means a weak pillar cannot be masked by a strong one.

Why it is credible

What makes the rating defensible

A maturity rating only has value if the market trusts it and it reflects the standards you will actually be assessed against.

Mapped to the standards you will be assessed against

Every pillar maps to India’s DPDP Act and IT Rules, ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF and the EU AI Act — so the rating doubles as a gap map toward certification and compliance.

A scored position, not a vanity number

Your score is built from 25 evidence-backed metrics across six pillars, weighted and non-compensatory, so you see exactly which pillars carry your rating and how far each one sits from the next tier.

Non-compensatory, with critical-control gates

Geometric scoring means heavy spend on infrastructure cannot mask weak governance. Fail a gate on AI security or agentic oversight and no certification is issued, whatever the total score.

We assess, and we do not sell the fix

AGBA does not sell the remediation it then grades. That separation is what keeps the rating defensible to a board, a customer or an acquirer.

Publicly checkable

Every certificate carries a number anyone can verify, with its scope and expiry. A rating that cannot be checked is marketing.

Mapped to what you will actually be assessed against

ISO/IEC 42001

The AI management system standard. We map your evidence to all nine Annex A control objective areas (A.2–A.10, 38 reference controls).

NIST AI RMF 1.0

Govern, Map, Measure, Manage — the risk vocabulary your global customers already use.

EU AI Act

High-risk obligations bind from Aug 2026. We map your readiness, not your legal conformity.

India DPDP Act

DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025, alongside the IT Act and IT Rules. Full compliance lands by May 2027.

What this is not. An AGBA rating is a conformity-readiness assessment. It is not a legal conformity assessment under the EU AI Act, nor an accredited ISO/IEC 42001 certification — those are granted only by the competent authority and an accredited certification body respectively. We map you to them, and we tell you what you would fail today.

FAQ

Questions buyers actually ask

If something here is unclear, it is a fault in how we explain it — tell us.

What does it cost?

The Readiness Rating is free at every size — a full scored report, your maturity level and a standards gap-map, with no card required. Verified Certification starts at ₹25,000 and scales with annual turnover, so a startup and a ₹1,000 crore group do not pay the same. See the fee ladder

How long is the certification valid?

12 months from the date of issue. After that the credential shows as expired on its public verification page and drops out of the public registry — AI readiness moves too fast for an open-ended claim to mean anything.

What is the difference between the free rating and the certification?

The rating is self-declared: you answer, the engine scores, you get the report. The certification adds independent verification — an assessor reviews a sample of your evidence before anything is issued — plus a verifiable badge and a listing in the public registry. Only the certification is a credential you can show a client.

What do you actually measure?

25 metrics across 6 pillars, mapped to ISO/IEC 42001, the NIST AI RMF, the EU AI Act and India’s DPDP Act. See the pillars or read the full published methodology — every weight and formula is on that page.

Can we just rate ourselves highly and pass?

No. Scoring is non-compensatory — a weak critical control caps your result no matter how strong everything else is — and a certification additionally requires that an assessor has verified evidence. A high self-rating with nothing behind it produces a rating, not a certification.

Who sees the result?

You do. Nothing is published unless you earn a certification and it is issued; only then does the credential appear on its verification page and in the public registry. The free rating is private to you.

What happens after we are certified?

You get the verifiable credential and badge, a listing in the public registry, and certified organisations are felicitated on stage at Bharat AI Innovation.
