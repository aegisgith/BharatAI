What two campuses learned about AI and jobs

Two panels on AI and Employability, at Dwarkadas J. Sanghvi College of Engineering in Mumbai and Jawaharlal Nehru University in New Delhi, with voices from Google, HDFC Life, IIT Delhi, JNU, Uno Minda, the Electronics Sector Skills Council and The Times of India. Each has become a free insights report: the findings, the panellists’ own examples, and an agenda for action for students, educators, employers and policymakers.
